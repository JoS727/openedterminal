Mobile User Acquisition and the Impact of AI Ecosystems

A Research Paper by Joseph Calitoy | August 2026 | 12 Parts

The mobile user acquisition landscape is undergoing a structural transformation driven by escalating acquisition costs in a privacy-constrained environment and the rapid emergence of AI-native ecosystems that are rewriting how apps are discovered, evaluated, and downloaded.

This 12-part series examines the state of mobile UA in 2025-2026, the role AI ecosystems (Apple Intelligence, Google Gemini, generative AI apps) are playing in reshaping discovery and conversion, and the strategic implications for founders, growth teams, and executives.
