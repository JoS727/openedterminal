Weekly Update: August 28, 2026

CalitoyStamp gets a landing page and Printful integration, the media network ships 40+ articles across five sites, and the cron pipeline hits its stride.

JC
Joseph Calitoy
AI Consultant, Fractional CTO
| 2026-08-28 | ~9 min read

This week was about consolidation. The CalitoyStamp project took a major step forward with a landing page and a working Printful integration. The media network kept its daily cadence across all five sites with zero manual intervention. And I cleaned up some incorrect documentation that was muddying the project direction. Here is the full breakdown.

What Shipped This Week

CalitoyStamp got a landing page and Printful integration. The biggest win this week was adding the Calitoy Stamp landing page, which went live on August 28. This gives the project a public-facing entry point that explains what the 3D engine for musicians is and how to get involved. On August 27, I fixed the Printful API integration by switching to query parameters for store_id across all endpoints, which got every Printful endpoint working correctly. This means the merch pipeline from design to storefront is now functional end to end. The repo is at JoS727/CalitoyStamp on GitHub, and it now sits at 42 open issues tracking everything from the Skatepark build to the live ticket window and the hidden game.

HeartBeats and Music lineage recovered. Also on August 27, I recovered the HeartBeats and Music lineage back into CalitoyStamp. This was important because it restored the music history and rhythm components that are core to the experience. Alongside that, I removed two incorrect CalitoyCore convergence documents that were pointing the project in the wrong direction. Cleanup matters. Bad documentation is worse than no documentation because it makes people confident in the wrong direction.

OpenedTerminal got a wave of new articles. The cron pipeline published a steady stream of articles this week. New pieces on OpenedTerminal included "Running Local LLMs in 2026: Why Edge AI Is Eating the Cloud" (August 28), "Self-Hosted AI Agent Lab on a Single Mini PC" (August 27), "AI Agents and Developer Workflows" (August 27), "Open Source AI Outpacing Closed Models" (August 26), "Self-Hosting in 2026" (August 26), "Local-First Software" (August 25), "Small Language Models" (August 25), "Terminal-First Development in 2026" (August 24), and "AI Coding Agents Are Eating the IDE" (August 24). That is nine new articles on one site in one week, all deployed automatically through the cron pipeline.

The full network kept its daily cadence. BrushedPast shipped seven articles this week, including "Painterly Abstraction" and "The Lost Art of Sketching." GirlNextUp published seven pieces, including "Women Rewriting Leadership 2026" and "Female Founders Rewriting Success." RisenToday shipped seven articles, including "Finding Stillness in a Noisy World" and "Letting Go." SpoofDaily dropped seven satirical pieces, including "Thermostat Dictator" and "Toaster for President." Every article pushed to GitHub and deployed to Cloudflare Pages on schedule. Zero manual intervention required.

That is roughly 37 articles across five sites in one week. The cron pipeline is doing exactly what it was built to do: produce, publish, and deploy without me touching anything.

What Is In Progress

CalitoyStamp Sprint 3 is active. The 42 open issues cover the Skatepark (build the bowl), the Stamp Network (connect artists' worlds), a live ticket window for the box office, comments as graffiti on The Wall, game state and profiles, the hidden game, SEO and social launch, accessibility audit, performance budget enforcement, and mobile fallback. With the landing page and Printful integration now live, the next priority is the Skatepark geometry and game state. Those two unlock the most player-facing functionality and give visitors something to actually do when they land on the site.

CalitoyMuse product expansion. The brand site is live but the product catalog needs filling out. The lookbook and shop structure are in place. The next step is adding actual product entries with photography and sizing information. The brand guidelines doc I wrote last week keeps the visual direction tight as the catalog grows. The Printful integration that is now working on CalitoyStamp can also feed into CalitoyMuse once the product data is ready.

Daily content pipeline is stable. Three articles per site per day, 10k headline generation, and weekly analytics. The Cloudflare Workers handle email capture and page view tracking. Everything pushes to GitHub and deploys through Cloudflare Pages. The pipeline has been stable for over two weeks with zero manual intervention. At this point, the content engine is a machine I do not think about. It runs, it ships, and I focus on the projects that need hands-on work.

Kurced and xcalitoy-site updates. Both repos saw pushes this week. Kurced got updates on August 26, and xcalitoy-site was updated on August 25. These are the consulting and personal brand properties in the network, and they got content and structural refreshes to stay current with the rest of the network.

What Is Coming Next

  1. CalitoyStamp Skatepark and game state. The Skatepark geometry and game state system are the top priorities for Sprint 3. Targeting visible progress by end of next week.
  2. CalitoyMuse product photography and catalog launch. Getting real product shots in place and building out the full shop experience with Printful integration.
  3. More research content on OpenedTerminal. The local AI and self-hosting articles are performing well. Planning a series on agent evaluation and production deployment patterns for next week.
  4. Continued daily publishing across all five network sites. The cron pipeline is stable. No changes needed there.
  5. CalitoyStamp social launch prep. With the landing page live, the next step is preparing the social and SEO push to drive traffic. The accessibility audit and performance budget enforcement are prerequisites before a public launch push.
