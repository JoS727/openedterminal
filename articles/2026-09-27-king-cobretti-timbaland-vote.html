King Cobretti Enters the Timbaland Global Search

Technical breakdown: prizes, voting mechanics, and fan prize distribution

OT
Opened Terminal Staff
September 27, 2026

King Cobretti, originally from San Jose, now based in San Diego, has entered the Timbaland x Orbiiit Global Talent Search 2026. The competition offers $100,000 in cash prizes, a studio master class session with Timbaland, a live showcase at Timbaland x Orbiiit, and a chance to be featured on Timbaland's next album.

Cobretti's audio entry is live. His bio reads: "Originally from San Jose, Ca. Now living in San Diego Ca. California is my home. And Music is my life, Im ready to cook and inspire."

Contest Mechanics

The Timbaland x Orbiiit Global Talent Search is open to artists from every genre, every country, and every background. Ten winners will be invited to the live show where five artists will secure spots on Timbaland's next album.

Every vote a participant receives also earns their supporters fan prize entries — meaning when you vote for King Cobretti, you are not just helping an artist, you are also entering yourself to win prizes like studio sessions, showcase invitations, and album features.
